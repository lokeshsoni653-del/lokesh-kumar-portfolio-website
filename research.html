Bridging the Digital Literacy Gap in Rural Agri-Logistics: A Trilingual, Context-Aware Intelligent Driver Assistance System (Agri-Logistics IDAS)

Authors: Lokesh Kumar (Student Member, IEEE | ORCID: 0009-0006-6510-6393 ↗) & Prof. Dr. Bhawani Shankar Chowdhry (Distinguished National Professor, Life Senior Member, IEEE)
Affiliations: Dept. of Software Engineering, Sindh Agriculture University (SAU) & Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET)
Field Partner: Strengthening Participatory Organisation (SPO), Pakistan (spopk.org ↗)
Executive Abstract

Rural agricultural logistics in developing regions suffer catastrophic post-harvest losses (up to 30–40% in perishable produce) driven by informal transport networks, lack of real-time route optimization, and digital illiteracy among vernacular-speaking drivers. Existing commercial telematics solutions presuppose literacy, high-bandwidth connectivity, and standardized road maps—assumptions that fail in desert and remote agricultural corridors.

This research designs, implements, and empirically validates the Agri-Logistics Intelligent Driver Assistance System (IDAS)—a cyber-physical edge-computing framework offering context-aware routing and offline vernacular voice guidance in Dhatki, Sindhi, and Urdu.

Empirical Methodology & Field Results

Over field transit trials across a 1,313-point route corridor in Lower Sindh, true-road OSRM graph traversal corrected a 17.07% Haversine underestimation error (paired t-test: t = 4.892, p = 0.0017). The edge voice co-pilot delivered sub-second response times (747.92 ms turnaround) strictly conforming to ISO 26262 automotive safety thresholds, with a 0.0034 ms latency for Dhatki dialect intent classification on low-cost edge microprocessors.

Economic modeling demonstrated an immediate elimination of unbudgeted diesel burn (-8.84 Liters / Rs. 2,488.81 PKR per round trip) while modeling route-delay reductions around the 19.2°C thermal alert threshold to help mitigate preventable transit-induced spoilage (live in-crate crop decay scheduled for Phase 4 field trials).

Dhatki & Sindhi Vernacular Agricultural Logistics Intent Corpus & Edge NLP Architecture

Hugging Face Repository: lokeshsonara/dhatki-agri-intents ↗
Target Dialects: Dhatki (Tharparkar desert vernacular) & Regional Sindhi
License & Format: MIT Open-Source License · Apache Parquet Indexed
Hardware Target: Fully offline microcontroller & Raspberry Pi Zero 2W inference
Problem & Scientific Challenge

Standard ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) systems like OpenAI Whisper, Google Speech API, and Wav2Vec2 are trained predominantly on high-resource languages and require continuous high-speed cloud connectivity. In contrast, Dhatki is an Indo-Aryan desert dialect with zero standardized orthography, no pre-existing digital corpus, and spoken in transit zones with zero cellular connectivity.

Dataset Corpus & Edge Optimization

1. Parallel Multilingual Corpus: Compiled and structured a domain-specific agricultural logistics intent dataset spanning Dhatki, Sindhi, Urdu, and English dispatch phrases with phonetic transcriptions.
2. INT8 Quantized Classifier: Distilled an acoustic intent classification model quantized to 8-bit integer weights, achieving an ultra-compact footprint (< 2.4 MB) suitable for real-time edge RAM.
3. Sub-Millisecond Edge Inference: Benchmarked on embedded hardware with a latency of 0.0034 ms per intent classification inference, enabling immediate driver voice commands (e.g. route detour, thermal alert acknowledgement) without cloud round-trips.
